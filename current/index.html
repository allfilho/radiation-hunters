How to Play

Movement

Use arrow keys or WASD to move. Hold SHIFT to run (uses stamina).

Geiger Counter

Press G to toggle the Geiger counter. Higher readings mean you're closer to a radioactive source!

Shields

Press 1-4 to change shields. Different radiation types are blocked by different materials:

  • Alpha (α): Blocked by anything - air, paper, aluminum
  • Beta (β): Blocked by aluminum and acrylic
  • Gamma (γ): Only lead provides significant attenuation

Identification

Get close to a source and press Q to identify it. Use your shield readings to determine the radiation type!

Settings

Without encounters and vault, the maximum overall score is 75%.
